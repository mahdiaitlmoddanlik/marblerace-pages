# Privacy Policy Last updated: October 2026 MarbleRace is a personal, single-user application. It is used only by its owner. ## Data accessed The app requests the YouTube upload permission (youtube.upload) through Google OAuth. It uses this permission only to upload videos the owner created to the owner's own YouTube channel. ## Data collected and stored The app does not collect personal data from any other users. The OAuth access token is stored locally on the owner's computer and is never transmitted to any third party or server. ## Sharing No data is sold, shared or disclosed to third parties. No analytics or advertising is used. ## Retention and deletion The owner can delete the stored token at any time and can revoke access at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions ## Contact mahdi.aitlmoddan@gmail.com
